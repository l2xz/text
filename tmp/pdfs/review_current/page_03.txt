3.3 Client-Adaptive Curriculum Construction
A fixed curriculum may be unsuitable under client heterogeneity
because the same nominal difficulty can produce different learn-
ing signals for different client policies.FedCAREtherefore sepa-
rates candidate availability from candidate ranking. The availability
schedule progressively exposes easy, medium, and eligible local
hard pairs, whereas ranking is recomputed independently by each
client in every round.
